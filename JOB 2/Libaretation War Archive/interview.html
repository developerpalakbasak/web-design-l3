Interviews

Collection of recorded interviews and testimonies from individuals who witnessed the 1971 Liberation War.

Footage & Voices of Liberation War 1971

Interviewee / Speaker: Veterans & Freedom Fighters (Mukti Bahini)

Role: Frontline Guerilla Fighters & Civilian Eyewitnesses

Testimony Summary: Firsthand recollections describing Sector 2 and Sector 11 military engagements, civilian resistance movements across rural outposts, and the pivotal turning points during the liberation struggle.

Rare Historical Footage & Eyewitness Accounts

Interviewee / Speaker: International War Correspondents & Civilian Survivors

Role: Journalists & Displaced Families of 1971

Testimony Summary: Recorded testimonies detailing the refugee crisis across borders, humanitarian initiatives, and the decisive joint allied operations that led to the surrender on 16 December 1971.
